Local Architecture

How Blackout Works

1. Ingest Locally

Paste unstructured text or import local .txt / .csv files up to 5 MB. Files are read strictly in local RAM via the HTML5 File API.

2. Detect & Validate

Our prioritized engine parses text against 8 detector rules. Numbers are validated with Luhn and Verhoeff dihedral checksums to discard decoys.

3. Sanitize & Export

Switch between Full Mask, Partial Mask, or Pseudonymization. Compare diffs and export in one click using client-side in-memory Blobs.

Deterministic Recognition

Supported PII Categories

EMAIL
Email Addresses

RFC local parts, @ symbol, valid domain names, and TLDs of 2+ letters with word boundaries.

CARD
Payment Cards

13–19 digits validated with the Luhn Checksum algorithm. Repeated decoy strings are rejected.

AADHAAR
Aadhaar Identifiers

12 digits starting 2–9 validated via Verhoeff dihedral group $D_5$ permutation tables.

PAN
Indian Tax PAN

[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z] validated against official 4th-character tax entity holder codes (P, C, H, F, A, T, etc.).

IP
IP Addresses

Dotted-quad IPv4 addresses with 0–255 octet bounds and full eight-group colon-separated IPv6 addresses.

PHONE
Phone Numbers

10-digit Indian mobiles starting 6–9 with +91/0091/0 prefixes, and international E.164 phone numbers.

Limitations & Responsible Use

Blackout detects structured patterns using regex and mathematical checksums. Unstructured contextual entities (such as person names or street addresses) require NLP/NER models and are out of scope. Pattern-based redaction reduces data exposure risks; it does not constitute a legal guarantee of full data anonymization.
